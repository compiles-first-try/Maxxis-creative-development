MARS TERRAFORM

You're stranded in a station orbiting a dead, barren Mars. Fire the terraforming lasers to warm the planet — then drop to the surface and bring it to life.
🔋 Find the 2 batteries floating around the station  (walk up, press F)
🎒 Open your inventory with E — drag a battery into the ✋ hand slot
⚡ Feed both into the Power Box (press F at the box)
🔴 Click the glowing button — lasers fire, Mars heats up
